Community Service Project Successfully Completed

Admin 22 July 2026 Club News 1,245 Views

Serving Humanity Through Action

Rotaract Club of Cumilla South successfully organized a community service project with enthusiastic participation from members, volunteers, and local community leaders. The initiative focused on supporting underprivileged families while strengthening fellowship and leadership among members.

The event included food distribution, educational support, environmental awareness, and healthcare assistance. Members worked together to ensure that every activity created a positive and lasting impact on the community.

"Service Above Self is not just a motto—it is our way of life."

The project also inspired new volunteers to join future initiatives. RAC SOUTH TIMES will continue highlighting stories of service, leadership, and positive change through every edition of the magazine.
